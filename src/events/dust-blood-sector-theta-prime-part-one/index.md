## CAPTIVES
As this is a basic Necromunda campaign and to avoid having to play extra games we may not be able to play in the time available and gangs who have fighters captured follow the following rules:

- Check with opponent what they are doing with the fighter they have captured they choose from.
- Return for a fee, agree a value in credits and adjust both gangs credits stash and return fighter to action.
- Sell fighter to local guild/mine/work house, add a quarter of the fighters cost/value (rounding up) and add that value to your credits stash. Fighter is removed from gang rosters.
- Keep kit and dospose of the body, add the fighters eqiptment excluding pets (they run off) to your stash and remove the figher from the roster.
- No option to rescue a captured fighter is available in this campaign.
