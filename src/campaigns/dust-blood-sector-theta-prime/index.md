This 3-month introductory campaign is designed to blood your fresh gangers, teach you how to haul back precious credits, show you how to patch up your wounded before they rot in the gutter, spend those credits to upgrade your gang, invest your hard-earned XP into lethal fighter upgrades, and ruthlessly deal with any captured rival gang members.

## The Sector Theta-Prime Campaign Overview
Over the next three months, rival gangs will clash across three distinct scenarios: Border Dispute, Smash & Grab, and The Trap. After each battle, players will follow the rulebook to log individual fighter Experience (XP), spend loot gained from battles to upgrade their gang's gear, patch up their wounded gang members, and decide the grim fate of any captured enemies.

The final standing of the campaign will be dictated entirely by each gang's final rating. At the end of Month 3, the player whose gang has achieved the highest Gang Rating will be declared the undisputed champion of the sector, with all other players ranked accordingly based on their final ratings.
